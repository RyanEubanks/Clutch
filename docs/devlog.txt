One guide warns that Ollama defaults Gemma 4 to a 4K context window,
 even though the model supports 128K to 256K, and that you 
 should override num_ctx for long inputs. That doesn’t matter 
 for short chats, so ignore it for now. It will matter 
 when you start putting whole notes or textbook chapters into the prompt, 
 because the model might otherwise cut off the end without telling you.
